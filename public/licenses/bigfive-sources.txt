Adaptation: English wording, original IDs, domains, keyed direction and facets
are preserved. The upstream Chinese translation was reviewed against English;
our displayed Chinese wording is a local plain-language adaptation, not a
validated Chinese instrument. No population norms or diagnostic claims are made.
The 120-item O6 political items retain source content and may be skipped as
not applicable. Do not treat skipped items as neutral. Our local completeness
policy requires 80% of domain items and 3 of 4 facet items. This policy and the
linear 0–100 display are application choices, not validated source norms.
The 50-item and 120-item instruments differ: scores are not interchangeable.
120 items have 30 facets; 50 items do not supply facet scores.
Charges in this product are for the report and consultation service, not
exclusive ownership of the public-domain item pool.
