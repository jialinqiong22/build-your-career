Additional instruments: sourcing and limitations
Reviewed 2026-09-06

Shipped Holland module: the project's existing 18 ORIGINAL Chinese exploration
items, retained verbatim with existing IDs. They refer to the general RIASEC
framework, not to a copied commercial questionnaire. They are not the requested
90-item standard/full version and have no established psychometric validation.
Scores are linear within-scale response intensity, never norms or percentiles.

Shipped Enneagram module: 36 ORIGINAL Chinese motivation-reflection prompts
written for this project after the user's acceptance of an original alternative
on 2026-09-06. Four prompts per theme, interleaved. The interpretation prompts
are also original. No RHETI or OEPS items are copied. This is NOT a classic,
standard, or validated Enneagram instrument. Scores show agreement with these
prompts, not diagnosed types or hidden fears. It is optional and does not enter
core personal-positioning calculations. The optional reference link does not
send this app's answers to the third party. External sites have their own terms.
https://enneagram-personality.com/zh-Hans
https://enneagram-personality.com/en/test/terms

Sources investigated but NOT copied into this product:

1. Interest Item Pool / RIASEC Markers.
https://jrounds.weebly.com/riasec-markers-scalesitems.html
The authors' current specific permission statement grants free noncommercial
use, despite the general site and older publications calling the pool public
domain. Because commercial reuse is not clearly granted by the current source,
the 48-item bank was not imported. Site lists update date 09/10/2023.

2. O*NET(R) Interest Profiler 60-item Short Form.
https://www.onetcenter.org/IP.html
https://www.onetcenter.org/license_tools.html
https://www.onetcenter.org/license_toolsdev.html
Verbatim redistribution: CC BY-ND 4.0. Translation/content modification:
O*NET Tools Developer License section 3(b)(2) requires a validation study prior
to introducing the new product. No such study has been performed for this app.
Therefore no Chinese translation or adapted questionnaire was shipped.

3. Open Enneagram of Personality Scales, Eric Jorgenson.
https://openpsychometrics.org/tests/OEPS/development/
https://openpsychometrics.org/development/
The development index refers generically to a Creative Commons license but the
specific license and commercial adaptation permission could not be established
from the reviewed materials. Version 1.0 downloadable ODT has 36 items; latest
development page updated 18 January 2026 describes 57, while the entry page
describes 54. No items imported, and no inaccurate claim of 'latest' made.

4. GitHub nthmost/rheti-python.
https://github.com/nthmost/rheti-python
MIT repository metadata is not sufficient to establish reuse rights for the
RHETI questionnaire reproduced from its original PDF. No content imported.

Completion times for original modules are estimates that need pilot testing.
No psychometric validation or commercial scale equivalence is claimed.
These notes record product implementation choices, not a legal opinion.
